\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Neural receivers jointly learn channel estimation, equalization, and
demapping and can improve \gls{BLER} over conventional pipelines
\cite{scamnrx,deeprx_mimo_icc}. A standard-compliant real-time
implementation has been demonstrated \cite{wiesmayr2024design}.
Model-driven variants reduce computation and parameters
\cite{mahdi_mdx}. Fine-tuning on 5G~NR measurements mitigates
sim-to-real mismatch \cite{nuri_reinhard_spawc}. Over-the-air tests
show simulation-trained receivers transfer to single-antenna
software-defined radios and improve with site-matched training
\cite{riku_ota}. \glspl{GPU} provide adaptive real-time RAN compute,
but a reported neural receiver requires
$\sim26$\,TFLOP/s, motivating quantization to reduce storage, traffic,
and bit-operation complexity \cite{ran_accelerated_compute_reinhard, park_simd}.

Prior integer weight-only \gls{QAT} for a \gls{SIMO} convolutional
receiver substantially improves over \gls{PTQ}, but uses uniform grids
and full-precision activations \cite{saketh_qat}. That study omits
post-\gls{ReLU} activation quantization, unstructured pruning, floating-point microformats, and standard-compliant
\gls{MU}-\gls{MIMO} reception. We compress the standard-compliant
convolutional graph neural receiver from prior work
\cite{scamnrx,wiesmayr2024design}. At the same nominal 4-bit weight
budget, OCP E2M1
(FP4) places reconstruction levels more densely near zero, whereas
INT4 uses uniform bins \cite{ocp2023mx}. We analyze whether this advantage
persists under INT8 activation quantization or global pruning and
analyze the resulting residual-kernel sparsity.

Our contributions are threefold:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Adding INT8 post-\gls{ReLU} activation quantization to 8-bit
    weight-\gls{QAT} models changes the required $E_b/N_0$ by at most
    $0.08$\,dB.
  \item At 4~bits, FP4~(E2M1) outperforms INT4 before and after $50\%$
    unstructured pruning.
  \item An analytic model quantifies weight storage and bit-operations.
\end{itemize}
Weight--activation \gls{QAT} and pruning are evaluated separately.
