\section{Proposed Quantization-Aware MU-MIMO Neural Receiver}
\label{sec:nrx_qat}

\subsection{Weight and Activation Quantization}
\label{sec:act_quant}
Every convolutional and dense kernel---depthwise, pointwise,
aggregation, and readout---uses \eqref{eq:fake_quant} with the INT8,
INT4, FP8~(E4M3) \cite{micikevicius2022fp8}, or FP4~(E2M1) grids of
Section~\ref{sec:qat_pruning}; biases remain in \texttt{FP32}. For a
post-\gls{ReLU} activation tensor $\mathbf{a}$, nonnegativity allows
unsigned asymmetric INT8 to use all 256 codes over
$[0,\max(\mathbf{a})]$, with per-tensor scale
$\Delta_a=\max(\mathbf{a})/255$ \cite{jacob2018quantization}.
Network inputs, residual states, aggregation outputs, and LLR/channel
heads remain in \texttt{FP32}. Applying weight and activation
fake-quantization in the same forward pass allows adaptation to both
noise sources; W$b$A8 denotes weight precision $b$ with INT8
post-\gls{ReLU} activations.

\subsection{Unstructured Magnitude Pruning}
\label{sec:pruning}
After weight \gls{QAT} converges, global magnitude pruning is applied
to pointwise-convolution and aggregation/dense kernels; depthwise
kernels and the LLR readout remain dense
\cite{song_han_1,kuzmin2023pruning}. Let $\mathcal{T}$ be this
eligible coefficient set, $W_i$ its $i$th coefficient, and $M_i$ the
corresponding binary keep-mask entry. A global threshold $\tau$ sets
sparsity $\rho$ through
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:prune_mask}
  M_i=\mathds{1}_{\{|W_i|\ge\tau\}},\qquad
  W_i^{\mathrm{pr}}=M_iW_i .
\end{equation}
Here,
$\rho=|\{i\in\mathcal{T}:|W_i|<\tau\}|/|\mathcal{T}|$ is the fraction
of eligible coefficients removed by the global threshold. We use
$\rho=50\%$ and hold $\mathbf{M}$ fixed during recovery
fine-tuning by projecting
$\mathbf{W}\leftarrow\mathbf{M}\odot\mathbf{W}$ after every update,
where $\odot$ denotes element-wise multiplication.

\subsection{Training Protocol}
Weight-only \gls{QAT} warm-starts from the pretrained \texttt{FP32}
checkpoint following \cite{saketh_qat}. Each W$b$A8 model is
initialized from its corresponding weight-only checkpoint and
fine-tuned with both fake-quantizers active. Sparse models are formed
after weight \gls{QAT} and undergo fixed-mask recovery fine-tuning.
