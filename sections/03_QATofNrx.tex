\section{Proposed Quantization-Aware MU-MIMO Neural Receiver}
\label{sec:nrx_qat}

This section specifies how the CGNN of Section~\ref{sec:architecture}
is quantized and, optionally, sparsified. We apply per-channel weight
\gls{QAT} in INT8, INT4, FP8~(E4M3), and FP4~(E2M1), INT8 asymmetric
fake-quantization of post-\gls{ReLU} activations, and optional 50\%
unstructured magnitude pruning, without modifying the computational
graph. The objective is to reduce weight storage and activation
traffic while preserving link-level \gls{BLER}.

\subsection{Weight and Activation Quantization}
\label{sec:act_quant}
Every convolutional and dense kernel---depthwise, pointwise,
aggregation, and readout---is passed through the fake-quantizer
\eqref{eq:fake_quant} in INT8, INT4, FP8~(E4M3), or FP4~(E2M1). Among
4-bit exponent--mantissa allocations we use E2M1, the sole FP4 element
format of the OCP MXFP4 specification \cite{ocp2023mx}, rather than an
ad hoc 4-bit grid. Biases and linear (non-\gls{ReLU}) tensors remain
in \texttt{FP32}.

After each \gls{ReLU} the activation tensor is nonnegative, so we apply
\emph{asymmetric} uniform INT8 fake-quantization over the per-tensor
range $[0,\max(\mathbf{a})]$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:act_fq}
  \widehat{\mathbf{a}}
  = Q_{\mathrm{INT8}}^{\mathrm{asym}}(\mathbf{a}),
  \qquad
  \mathbf{a}=\mathrm{ReLU}(\cdot).
\end{equation}
Linear layers (residual state, aggregation outputs, and LLR /
channel-estimate heads) and the network input remain in
\texttt{FP32}; only post-\gls{ReLU} feature maps are quantized. This
matches integer inference paths that keep unsigned activations after
\gls{ReLU} while preserving full-precision residuals
\cite{jacob2018quantization,wu2020integer}. During \gls{QAT},
\eqref{eq:act_fq} is composed with \eqref{eq:fake_quant}, so the
network jointly adapts to weight and activation noise. We denote such
models by W$b$A8 (weight bit-width $b$, INT8 activations). Weight
quantization reduces the parameter store; activation quantization
reduces the dominant on-chip traffic of feature maps
\cite{micikevicius2022fp8,jacob2018quantization}.

\subsection{Unstructured Magnitude Pruning}
\label{sec:pruning}
After a converged \gls{QAT} checkpoint, let $\mathcal{T}$ collect the
eligible tensors (separable-convolution \emph{pointwise} kernels and
aggregation/dense kernels; depthwise kernels and the LLR readout are
left dense). A global threshold $\tau$ is chosen so that a target
fraction $\rho$ of coefficients in $\mathcal{T}$ satisfy $|w|<\tau$
\cite{song_han_1,kuzmin2023pruning}. With a binary keep-mask
$\mathbf{M}$ of matching shape,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:prune_mask}
  W^{\mathrm{pr}}_{i}
  = M_{i}\,W_{i},
  \qquad
  M_{i}=\mathds{1}_{\{|W_{i}|\ge\tau\}},
\end{equation}
we obtain unstructured sparsity with unchanged tensor shapes. In our
experiments $\rho=50\%$. The pruned model is then fine-tuned under
\eqref{eq:fake_quant}, with hard projection onto $\mathbf{M}$ after
each optimizer step so that pruned coordinates remain zero.

\subsection{QAT and Pruned Fine-Tuning}
\label{sec:qat_protocol}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:qat_prune} summarizes the pipeline. Every forward
pass evaluates the receiver on $N_B$ OFDM slots under fake-quantized
weights \eqref{eq:fake_quant} and, for W$b$A8 models, post-\gls{ReLU}
fake-quantization \eqref{eq:act_fq}; the \gls{STE} updates the
full-precision master copy. Weight-only checkpoints warm-start the
matching W$b$A8 fine-tunes. Optional magnitude pruning is applied only
after weight \gls{QAT} has converged, followed by a short masked
recovery fine-tune.

All stages use $N_B{=}128$ slots per optimizer step. FP8~(E4M3) and
FP4~(E2M1) \gls{QAT} run for
$N_{\mathrm{QAT}}=5{\times}10^{4}+2{\times}10^{5}$ iterations at
learning rates $10^{-4}$ then $5{\times}10^{-5}$
($3.2{\times}10^{7}$ simulated slots); INT8/INT4 use
$N_{\mathrm{QAT}}=10^{6}+9{\times}10^{6}$ iterations at $10^{-4}$
($1.28{\times}10^{9}$ slots). W$b$A8 fine-tunes continue from the
parent checkpoint under joint weight-and-activation fake-quantization
with the same optimizer schedule. Post-prune recovery uses
$N_{\mathrm{P}}=5{\times}10^{3}+10^{4}$ iterations at
$5{\times}10^{-5}$ then $2{\times}10^{-5}$
($1.92{\times}10^{6}$ additional slots), with hard mask projection
after every update. Weight-only, W$b$A8, and QAT$+$pruning checkpoints
are compared in Section~\ref{sec:results}.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Weight--activation QAT with optional magnitude pruning.}
\label{alg:qat_prune}
\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE \texttt{FP32} master weights $\mathbf{W}$; grid $\mathcal{G}$;
         $A\in\{\mathrm{off},\mathrm{A8}\}$; $N_{\mathrm{QAT}}$;
         optional $(\rho,N_{\mathrm{P}})$
\STATE Let $f_A$ denote $f$ with $F_{\mathcal{G}}$
       \eqref{eq:fake_quant} on all kernels and $Q_{\mathrm{A8}}$
       \eqref{eq:act_fq} enabled if $A=\mathrm{A8}$
\FOR{$t = 1$ \TO $N_{\mathrm{QAT}}$}
  \STATE Sample $(\mathbf{y},\mathbf{h},\mathbf{b})
         \sim\mathcal{D}^{N_B}$
  \STATE $(\hat{L},\hat{\mathbf{h}})
         \leftarrow f_A(\mathbf{y};F_{\mathcal{G}}(\mathbf{W}))$
  \STATE Update $\mathbf{W}$ with Adam and \gls{STE} on
         $\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{b},\hat{L},\hat{\mathbf{h}})$
\ENDFOR
\IF{$\rho > 0$}
  \STATE Build global mask $\mathbf{M}$ at sparsity $\rho$ on eligible kernels
         \eqref{eq:prune_mask};
         $\mathbf{W}\leftarrow\mathbf{M}\odot\mathbf{W}$
  \FOR{$t = 1$ \TO $N_{\mathrm{P}}$}
    \STATE Sample a batch; apply the quantized forward pass and
           Adam--\gls{STE} update
    \STATE Project
           $\mathbf{W}\leftarrow\mathbf{M}\odot\mathbf{W}$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDIF
\STATE \textbf{return} $\mathbf{W}^{\star}
       \leftarrow F_{\mathcal{G}}(\mathbf{W})$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
