\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{conclusion}
Joint weight--activation \gls{QAT}, optionally with 50\% unstructured
magnitude pruning, compresses a standard-compliant MU-\gls{MIMO}
5G~NR neural receiver with negligible radio cost at 8~bits. At
4~bits, FP4~(E2M1) remains superior to \gls{LS}--\gls{LMMSE} after
pruning, whereas INT4 does not; the gap tracks the concentration of
normalized weights near zero and the layer-wise sparsity induced by a
global magnitude threshold.

The evaluation is restricted to DoubleTDL-low at MCS~14 with
post-\gls{ReLU} activation quantization; richer MCS sets, higher
antenna correlation, and larger user loads are left to future work.
The trained E2M1 codebook already matches the MXFP4 element type
\cite{ocp2023mx}, so native MXFP4 inference is a packaging task.
Combining the grid with structured sparsity (e.g., 2:4) is a further
step toward memory- and compute-efficient neural uplink receivers for
\gls{6G} \gls{RAN}.
